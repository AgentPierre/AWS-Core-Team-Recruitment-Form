Why Join AWS Cloud Club?

As a Member

  • Build practical cloud skills through hands-on learning
  • Work on real projects that strengthen your portfolio
  • Learn from peers, mentors, and visiting speakers
  • Grow in a supportive and inclusive tech community

As a Core Team Leader

  • Lead initiatives, events, and technical programs on campus
  • Develop communication, planning, and team leadership skills
  • Help shape club strategy and mentorship for future leaders
  • Gain professional recognition through visible impact
  • AWS credits (when applicable)
  • Certification voucher opportunities (when applicable)
  • Swag and badges (when available)
